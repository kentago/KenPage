💍 Starting Your Run

At the start of each run you make two choices:

  1. Heirloom Ring — Pick 1 of 3 random starter rings. Each has unique stats and a trait that shapes your early game.
  2. Stat Boost — Pick one stat (STR/DEX/INT/CHA) to boost by +1. Green buttons, one-time choice.

⚔️ Combat & Flee

Enemies are gatekeepers — you must defeat them to pass through. If you flee successfully, you return to the previous room. The enemy stays alive, blocking that path until killed.

Flee damage scales with floor depth — fleeing deep is dangerous!

Enemies scale to your power. Because the dungeon is endless, monsters adapt to how strong you actually are — your gear, stats, and HP. No matter how powerful your weapon, foes are never a one-hit kill, and every floor stays a real fight. Likewise, no single enemy hit can ever one-shot you — but a bad fight can still be fatal, so keep an eye on your HP and use fountains and the doctor's healing sip when the risk gets close.

❤️ Watch the health border: the frame around the map (and your HP readout in its bottom-left corner) changes colour with your health — green when safe, yellow, then orange at half, red low, and flashing red when critical. When it flashes, flee or find healing fast!

⚔️ Damage per item: each piece of gear shows a small ⚔️ +N dmg/hit tag — how much that item adds to your average swing from its Strength alone. It's the raw, dependable damage (trait bonuses are not counted — those fire on top during a fight). Add up the tags across your gear to see your baseline hitting power, and compare pieces at a glance. Items with no Strength show no tag (they help through traits or other stats instead).

⚡ Traits always stack — nothing is ever wasted. Wearing the same trait on several items keeps making it stronger. Effects that add damage or healing simply pile up. Chance-based effects (like a double-strike, a dodge, or scaring a foe) climb higher with every extra copy — a lot at first, then by smaller and smaller steps — always rising toward a near-certainty but never quite reaching a guaranteed 100%. So chasing and rerolling for more copies of a trait always pays off a little more; you'll never hit a wall where an extra copy does nothing.

👑 Floor Bosses

Every 5th floor (5, 10, 15...) has a unique boss guarding the ladder down. Bosses are randomized each run — different name, element, abilities, and stats. Killing a boss gives 5-10× XP and drops a guaranteed legendary item.

🔥 Kill Streak

Kill enemies to build a kill streak. Each kill in the streak gives +10% bonus XP (up to 3× max). Your streak breaks if you: rest at a healing fountain, flee from a fight, or wander through 3 rooms in a row without finding an enemy (a lucky roll can stretch this to 4). Luck fountains do NOT break streaks. Keep hunting to keep the fire burning.

💍 Rings & Fingers

You have 10 ring slots across two hands: Left 🫲 (shield) and Right 🫱 (weapon). Rings are key equipment — many have synergy traits that get stronger the more rings you wear.

⚠️ Be careful! Traps and critical combat failures can permanently sever fingers. A lost finger means that ring slot is gone forever. Lose all 5 on your right hand and you can't wield weapons. Lose all 5 on the left and you take 50% more damage. Each finger lost makes the next one harder to lose (exponential protection).

🪜💦 Slippery rungs: once in a rare while a ladder is slick and your grip fails — a slip can cost you a finger. It doesn't happen often. A hand marked by old wounds (🩹 scarred fingers) is the most at risk, and wearing lots of rings worsens your grip a little too; a healthy bare hand is safest. As always, a lost scarred finger tends to go before a fresh one, and Finger Ward can still save you.

🪜 Ladders & Floors

Ladders are portals — each one leads to a unique position on the next floor. Two ladders on the same floor lead to different spots below (separate "islands" that may or may not connect). Yellow 🪜 = undiscovered, Green 🪜 = used. Ladders always go exactly one floor down/up.

If you're completely stuck (all rooms explored, all searched, no ladder found), a 🕳️ emergency escape shaft (red on the map) will open — a forced descent to the next floor. This is not the same as a 🌀 quick-travel portal: the escape shaft only drops you down one floor, while portals (gold 🌀) are saved teleport nodes you can travel to and from at will.

🚧 Toll to descend: going down a ladder costs a toll (boss ladders are free — the boss is the gate). The toll is set by how far you've come — how many tolls you've already paid this run and how deep you are — not by how much gold you're carrying. Every toll is a little higher than the one before, so it always climbs and never dips. At every 10th level the gatekeepers take a fresh look at your wealth and may re-price passage to match a growing fortune — but this can only raise the toll, never lower it. You can't just buy your way down — keep fighting and searching to afford deeper descents (selling loot helps). The price you see is the price you pay, and any ladder you've already paid stays free forever, so you can move back through cleared floors freely.

🔎 Searching

Each room can be searched once (d20 roll + INT bonus). Results: legendary items (20+), great loot (16-19), normal loot (10-15), nothing (6-9), traps (2-5, DEX can dodge), critical fail (1). Hidden ladders can also be discovered!

🌀 Secret passages & portals: searching can uncover a rare secret passage — a shimmering portal. There is at most ONE portal per floor, and they're uncommon (Luck raises the odds slightly), so you might find one on floor 1, another on floor 3, another on floor 7. Each portal is named after a periodic element and its floor, e.g. Helium Floor 1 Portal, Gold Floor 4 Portal. Every portal you find appears as a small purple box just beside the map — one box per portal, labelled with its floor. The boxes only show up as you discover portals, and they line up neatly so a keen searcher who finds one on many floors can keep them all within easy reach.

🌀 Quick travel: click a portal's 🌀 purple box to teleport to it instantly (across floors too). Your position is remembered — when you're standing on the portal, its box turns into ↩️ Back; click it to jump straight back to where you left from. Example: with a single portal, walk two rooms away, click its box to return to it, then click ↩️ Back to come back to that two-rooms-away spot. Great for stashing a bookmark before a risky detour. (You can't quick-travel while an enemy blocks the room.)

💡 Search grows more powerful mid-game onward. As you level (more INT) and descend (loot scales with floor, rarity odds shift toward Mythical/Legendary/Divine, higher depth borders unlock), searching becomes a primary source of build-defining gear. A natural 20 always yields a boosted named legendary. Early floors give modest finds — deep floors make thorough searching essential.

Searches may also turn up lucky trinkets (🍀 horseshoe, four-leaf clover, star fragment... — instant permanent Luck) or a mystery potion (🧪 — drink it to gamble: could be healing, luck, a stat boost... or poison. Or leave it untouched).

🔵 NPCs & Quests

NPCs give quests to find specific items on a specific floor. The quest UI tells you exactly where to search. You must deliver the correct item to the correct NPC. Deeper quests = better rewards (XP + items). CHA boosts quest XP by +5% per point.

🎨 Item Borders

Items have up to two independent borders. Most loot has none — plain items are common. When a border appears, it means something special:

  • Inner border (Rarity): Common (none) → Uncommon (blue) → Rare (orange) → Epic (pink) → Mythical (purple) → Legendary (red) → Divine (cyan)
  • Outer glow (Depth): Bronze (none) → Silver → Gold → Titan → Platinum → Glowing → Prismatic → Astral

The two axes are independent — a lucky early roll could give a purple Mythical on floor 1, or a plain item with a glowing depth ring. Deeper floors shift the odds toward higher tiers, so a floor-200 run sees far more Legendary/Divine and Prismatic/Astral gear. Rarity is NOT power — check the stats!

💲 Traders

Traders both buy and sell. Sell your loot for gold (CHA boosts sell prices +3%/point, capped), and buy their 3-5 wares if you can afford them. Traders size you up before laying out their goods: their wares scale to your current gear (so they never become useless), and with a bit of luck one item will be a guaranteed upgrade over something you have equipped — look for the ⭐ Trader's Pick. Beware: the richer you look, the greedier their prices get, so a fat purse means a steeper markup.

🔄 Clear the shelves for a restock: if you buy out a trader's entire stock, they'll offer to lay out a brand-new batch of wares for a restock fee. Each fresh batch is re-sized to your current gear (always relevant) with another shot at a ⭐ Trader's Pick. The restock fee is a slice of your gold and grows every time you restock — so a few restocks in a row will drain even a deep purse. This is the best way to spend a big hoard chasing the perfect item, and it keeps the trader parked in the dungeon as long as you keep buying.

💰 Gold

Earn gold by: killing enemies (more from bosses/elites), searching rooms (stashes), and selling to traders. Spend it buying gear from traders or paying doctors to restore lost fingers.

⚕️ Doctors

Doctors (⚕️) can surgically restore lost fingers — reopening ring slots permanently. Base price scales with floor depth, with a bulk discount (2nd finger 30% off, 3rd 50% off, more 60% off).

Doctors also offer a healing sip (💧 restores ~35% HP, no risk). It's cheap early when you're poor, but the price scales with your max HP — so at depth a full heal is a serious expense. Weigh it against selling gear or pushing on to find a fountain.

⚠️ But surgery is a d20 gamble: roll well and it works (a natural 20 even refunds half your gold). Roll a 1 and disaster strikes — a questionable doctor might pocket your gold and flee forever, botch the operation and cost you another finger, or jab you with a poisoned syringe. Choose your surgeon carefully.

💚 Fountains

Healing fountains (💚) restore HP (15-35% of max per sip). Luck fountains (🍀) grant permanent Luck stat. Each has limited sips (d20 determined). Every sip is a d20 roll:

  • 20 — Divine/massive effect (+50% heal or +3 Luck)
  • 17-19 — Great sip (+20% heal or +2 Luck)
  • 4-16 — Normal sip
  • 2-3 — Weak/no effect
  • 1 — ☠️ POLLUTED/CURSED! Heal fountains poison you; luck fountains drain Luck, may make you drop an equipped item (lost forever), and may wither a random base stat (worse the higher it is). 20% chance the fountain is permanently corrupted. Greedy sipping is dangerous!

🏆 Hall of Fame & Seasons

When you die (or give up a run), your run is submitted to the Global Hall of Fame. Compete worldwide! The admin resets seasons periodically — browse past seasons with the dropdown. Set a Tag to be recognized. Opt-in to show your country flag.

Ranking / tie-breaks: runs are ranked by XP first. If tied, by Level, then Floor reached, then Item count. If still identical, the run submitted earliest ranks higher. Only the top 10 worldwide are shown.

☠️ Permanent Death

When HP hits 0, the run ends forever. All buttons are disabled — you can screenshot your gear but cannot interact. Your legacy lives on in the Hall of Fame. Start fresh with New Run.

💜 Soul Keeper — an equipped item carrying this trait defies death: it is the one thing that survives into your next run. It comes back re-forged to level-1 power (a gentle head-start, never a snowball) and its Soul Keeper trait is spent in the process — so a retained heirloom cannot be kept a second time. Only equipped items qualify; for a normal slot that's a no-brainer. If the survivor is a ring, it takes the place of your opening ring choice — you begin the new run wearing it instead of picking a fresh starter ring.
