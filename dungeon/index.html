⚔️ Combat & Flee

Enemies are gatekeepers — you must defeat them to pass through. If you flee successfully, you return to the previous room. The enemy stays alive, blocking that path until killed.

Flee damage scales with floor depth — fleeing deep is dangerous!

🔥 Kill Streak

Kill enemies without resting at a healing fountain to build a kill streak. Each kill in the streak gives +10% bonus XP (up to 3× max). Resting at a heal source resets your streak. Luck fountains do NOT break streaks.

💍 Rings & Fingers

You have 10 ring slots across two hands: Left 🫲 (shield) and Right 🫱 (weapon). Rings are key equipment — many have synergy traits that get stronger the more rings you wear.

⚠️ Be careful! Traps and critical combat failures can permanently sever fingers. A lost finger means that ring slot is gone forever. Lose all 5 on your right hand and you can't wield weapons. Lose all 5 on the left and you take 50% more damage.

🪜 Ladders & Floors

Ladders let you descend to deeper floors. Yellow 🪜 = undiscovered, Green 🪜 = used (known connection). Each ladder always leads to the same spot. Explore a floor fully before descending — deeper floors are exponentially more dangerous!

Every 5th floor has a 👑 Boss guarding the ladder down.

🔎 Searching

Each room can be searched once. Results are a d20 roll: loot, traps, secret passages, quest items, or legendary finds on a natural 20. Critical fail (roll 1) can trigger traps, spawn enemies, or destroy items.

🔵 NPCs & Quests

NPCs give quests to find specific items. The quest tells you which floor to search on. Return the item to the correct NPC for XP + possible item rewards. Deeper quests give better rewards.

💲 Traders

Sell inventory items for gold at traders.

💚 Fountains

Healing fountains restore HP. Luck fountains (🍀) grant permanent Luck. Each has limited sips (d20 determined). Warning: roll a 1 and the fountain may be polluted/cursed — or permanently corrupted!

🏆 Hall of Fame

When you die, your run is submitted to the Global Hall of Fame. Compete worldwide across seasons! Set a Tag to be recognized. Opt-in to show your country flag.
