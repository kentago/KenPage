Narwhals vs Unicorns

A destructible-terrain duel. Everyone's got a horn — only one side wins.

Player 1 — Narwhal 🔵

  • A / D — move
  • W / S — aim up / down
  • Space — hold to charge, release to fire

Player 2 — Unicorn 🩷

  • ← / → — move
  • ↑ / ↓ — aim up / down
  • Enter — hold to charge, release to fire

Pick a weapon from your panel below the battlefield before you fire.

On a phone? Pass the device each turn — the on-screen pad below the battlefield always controls whoever's turn it is.
