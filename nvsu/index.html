Narwhals vs Unicorns

Two pods of three, a shrinking battlefield, and everyone's got a horn.

Player 1 — Narwhal Pod 🔵

  • A / D — move
  • W / S — aim up / down
  • Space — hold to charge, release to fire
  • Tab — switch which pod member you're controlling

Player 2 — Unicorn Pod 🩷

  • ← / → — move
  • ↑ / ↓ — aim up / down
  • Enter — hold to charge, release to fire
  • Tab — switch which pod member you're controlling

Pick a weapon from your panel below the battlefield before you fire.

On a phone? Pass the device each turn — the on-screen pad below always controls whoever's turn it is.
